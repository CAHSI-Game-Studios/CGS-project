= The Arcade Backstory

== Setting

* Location: Jovanni's Pizzeria, Seattle, Washington
* Main Setting: Inside the Starlight Gaming Co. Arcade Machine

== Main Character: Bernie Smith

* Name: Bernie Smith
* Age: Mid-30s
* Occupation: Technician at Al's Electric Repair Shop
* Description: An average Joe who took the job to make ends meet.
* Abilities:
** Basic electrical and circuit work
** High social skills
** Average luck (besides getting zapped)
* Weaknesses:
** Procrastinator
** Gets hungry easily
** Slow at completing tasks
* Personality:
** Relaxed
** Easygoing
** Street-smart
** Sociable

== What Is the Backstory Behind the Arcade?

Jovanni's Pizzeria, an old family establishment located on the outskirts of Seattle, Washington, has an old 1980s arcade game machine.

The machine was created by an old gaming technology company named Starlight Gaming Co. Over the years, it has traveled between various establishments around the world after mysteriously breaking down and refusing to function.

== Where Does the Story Begin?

After the Starlight Gaming Co. arcade machine breaks down at Jovanni's Pizzeria, the owner, Johnny Jr., calls Al's Electric Repair Shop for assistance.

The repair shop sends Bernie Smith, a new technician and average Joe who took the job to make ends meet.

What Bernie does not expect is that the machine will zap him into a new reality—one inside the Starlight Gaming Co. arcade machine.

== What Happens Inside the Arcade?

Once Bernie Smith (the player) is zapped inside the arcade machine, he must travel between different games to unlock tokens and hopefully find a way to return home.

Inside the arcade's *Hub*, which serves as the main game menu, Bernie encounters various original characters (OCs) from the different games. Some characters will help him, while others will hinder his progress.

=== Game Progression and Travel

The Arcade's main form of travel is through game portals.

* Bernie travels between games using portals accessible from the Hub.
* Each game presents different tasks and challenges that Bernie must complete.
* Completing tasks allows Bernie to unlock tokens.
* Bernie can also unlock game abilities that help him progress.
* Throughout his journey, Bernie interacts with different OCs who may help or hinder his progress.
* Bernie's ultimate goal is to find a way to escape the arcade machine and return to the real world.
