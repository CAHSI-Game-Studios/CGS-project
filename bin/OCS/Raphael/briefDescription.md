# OC Profile

## Basic Info
**Name:** Raphael
**Nickname / Alias:** -> The Chef
**Pronouns:** -> Any pronoun
**Role / Occupation:** ->  Head Chef in the Hub's inn/restaurant/bar

## Physical Description
Floating big feline-like eye with pink iris, sorrounded by Gemma's rings perpetually moving.

## Displayed Personality
It likes to hear people's story and it's really talkative itself. Likes to take care of people and cook them their favorite food any time they can. Unexistent sense of humor, almost acting robotic. Very friendly towards anyone at first impression. Prefers to be called "The Chef"

**A few personality words:** 
curious, intelligent, reserved, optimist

## World of Origin
It is a 9-Dimensional being from the planet Ouroboros, planet which is habitated by humans and by 9-Dimensional beings venerated as "Angels". Constantly in a warzone, tired of this war it eloped in search for a place where it can help people with it's craft.

**World / Setting Name:** Ouroboros

**Brief Description:**  
Planet that counts with a sun and a moon. Half of the planet has constant sunlight while the other half have constant moonlight. Each side has it's own singular goverment which is constantly battling for control over the side. The "angels" are just mere spectators of the war while also function as neutral messenger of both sides.

**One notable thing about their world:**  
Being an "angel" it had to represent itself in a way that 2-dimensional characters could comprehend.
