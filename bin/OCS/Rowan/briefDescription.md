# OC Profile

## Basic Info
**Name:** Rowan  
**Nickname / Alias:** Rowan     
**Pronouns:** she/her   
**Role / Occupation:** witch apprentice  

## Physical Description
A short description of what the character looks like.

- General appearance: average height for her age (late teens), tan skin, freckles
- Clothing / style: whimcsical yet practical clothes that allow movement, messenger bag for foraging
- Distinctive features: curly, dark purple hair (grows this color)

## Displayed Personality
How does the character usually come across to other people?

- General demeanor: active, cheerful, impatient, always eager to learn more about witchcraft
- Social habits: chatty and fast-talking
- Sense of humor: witty and dorky; she's a sucker for puns
- Common attitude toward strangers: polite but can be percieved as too hyperactive at times

**A few personality words:** curious, active, energetic, chatty

## World of Origin
A very short description of where the character comes from.

**World / Setting Name:** Hazelgrove 

**Brief Description:**  
Hazelgrove is a secluded village hidden in the heart of a forest populated mostly by witches. Its aesthetic is gentle and rustic, with wooden cottages, turf-covered roofs, and warm lanterns glowing through windows and around town.

**One notable thing about their world:**  
People outside of Hazelgrove aren't fond of witches, so they rather live in isolation. Rowan has never left the village, let alone met anyone outside, so everything in The Arcade feels foreign to her.

## Dialogue Flavor
Optional, but useful for interactions.

**Speaking style:** casual and energetic

**Common mannerism or habit:** rocks back and forth when still for too long

**Something they might say:**
> "These will go GREAT in my new potion!"

## First-Impression Hooks
A few things other characters can naturally notice, ask about, or react to.

- She loves to forage, so her bag is typically full of food, medicinal plants, supplies for witchcraft, and different types of mushrooms.
- Attached to her bag strap are multiple, small potion bottles.
- Within Hazelgrove she may be asked about progress in her witchcraft training.
- Sometimes she speaks so quick her words jumble and may be hard to make out, especially when excited.

## Creator Notes
Anything collaborators should know when writing scenes involving this character.

**Okay to improvise:** Reactions to The Arcade and other characters, dialogue and interactions, and minor preferences/decisions may be improvised, while bearing in mind her defined personality. Bonus points if there are jokes and fun facts related to witchcraft and mushrooms sprinkled about in her dialogue.

**Please avoid changing/revealing:** Why her hair is purple, general appearance, motivations, backstory, and goals should not be changed. She must always have her messenger bag and her hair should always be down unless she is potionmaking. More details pertaining to her lore will be revealed as the project progresses.

### Keep Some Cards Hidden 

You **do not need to include**:

- Full backstory
- Secret abilities
- Major relationships
- Character arcs
- Trauma or major past events
- Hidden motives
- Plot twists
- Complete lore

Those are more fun to discover through conversations and interactions inside **The Arcade**.
