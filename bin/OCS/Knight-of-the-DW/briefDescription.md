# OC Profile

## Basic Info
**Name:** Unknown
**Nickname / Alias:** The Knight of the Dead Weight
**Pronouns:** he/him
**Role / Occupation:** Wandering knight

## Physical Description
A lone knight who looks like he walked out of a war nobody else survived.

- General appearance: Tall, moving with the slow, careful weight of someone who is always tired. He is missing his left arm; the empty sleeve or bare shoulder is left as is, with no prosthetic.
- Clothing / style: Worn, battle-scarred knight's armor with a closed helm, covered by a faded, frayed black hood. A standard sword hangs at his hip, and an enormous greatsword is strapped to his back.
- Distinctive features: The greatsword. It is far too large to be practical, and he almost never draws it, yet he never leaves it behind. A grey wolf is always at his side.

## Displayed Personality
- General demeanor: Quiet, guarded and worn down. He seems to carry something heavy that has nothing to do with his sword.
- Social habits: Keeps to himself and avoids crowds. Rarely starts conversations, but listens more than he lets on.
- Sense of humor: Dry and rare. Usually so understated that people aren't sure he was joking.
- Common attitude toward strangers: Tells them to keep their distance. If someone is in trouble, he helps anyway, silently and without expecting thanks.

**A few personality words:**
`reserved, tired, blunt, protective, quietly kind`

## World of Origin
**Brief Description:**
A dark medieval world of crumbling kingdoms, fading faiths and lands scarred by old wars. Magic exists, but not everyone is born with it, and the kind of magic a person has shapes how the world treats them. It is a place where knights outlive their oaths, and legends are more often warnings than stories of heroes.

**One notable thing about their world:**
Rare forms of magic are feared as omens. Darkness magic in particular is considered a sign of ill fortune. Rarer still is the magic of the Abyss, a deeper and far more dangerous form born from darkness itself. Only one person in history is known to have wielded it, and everyone knows the legend of what they did with it.

## Dialogue Flavor
**Speaking style:**
`blunt / quiet / tired`: short, dry sentences, but never cruel or ill-intentioned.

**Common mannerism or habit:**
Glances at the wolf before answering, as if checking with it. Rests his hand on the greatsword's strap when uneasy.

**Something they might say:**
> "This sword is too valuable to use against you."
> "My wolf is not a pet; he is a companion."
> "I didn't choose this alias; everyone just started identifying me that way."

## First-Impression Hooks
- He carries a massive greatsword he almost never uses, which earned him his mocking alias.
- He is missing an arm, yet fights without any prosthetic.(I was thinking about the protagonist having some kind of magic that could conjure an arm whenever he needed it, not an arm made of flesh and blood, but one made of magic.)
- A grey wolf follows him everywhere and seems to understand him perfectly.
- He never gives his name, only the alias others gave him.
- He tells everyone to stay away, but keeps helping them anyway.
