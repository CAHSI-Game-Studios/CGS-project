## Physical Description
A short description of what the character looks like.


- General appearance:Tanned skin, Dark Bluish Gray hair that almost looks black and has the appearance of being struck by lightning. Eyes are gray with what appear to be moving sparks inside them, at times his eyes glow golden.

- Clothing / style: Typically seen wearing his armor which is cybernetic with gray being the main color, teal contrasts and details in yellow. In the chestplate, the armor contains the Tekmathian emblem. The armor appears to be chipped and cracked in different areas.

- Distinctive features:
His right eye has a lightning shaped scar starting from his eyebrow making the eyebrow look like a storm cloud. Wields a shapeshifting sword, mainly uses it in partizan(spear) form. Is able to control and generate lightning.

Due to previous conflicts caused by the nature of the Tekmathians (Lightning tribe), these same Tekmathians live in the clouds in the sky seperated from the other countries.
