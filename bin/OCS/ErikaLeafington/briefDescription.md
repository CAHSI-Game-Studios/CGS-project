# OC Profile

## Basic Info

**Name:** Erika Leafington
**Nickname / Alias:** Lady Leafington or Ms. Leafington
**Pronouns:** she/her
**Role / Occupation:** Daughter of a Earl

## Physical Description

A short description of what the character looks like.

- General appearance: A 26 year-old English noble lady with strong Arab heritage.
- Clothing / style: She wears gowns with nature inspired elements.
- Distinctive features: Long black hair and siren eyes. Over the top floral/nature inspired jewelry and accessories. Always carries a big hand-held fan.

> Keep this brief. Think "what another character would notice after meeting them," not a complete anatomical inventory.

## Displayed Personality

How does the character usually come across to other people?

- General demeanor: Upon first impression she can look stern. Makes sure to always maintain her composure.
- Social habits: Attends social gatherings out of obligation, but prefers solitude. Dislikes entertaining superficial conversations.
- Sense of humor: Likes to gossip and make sarcastic remarks.
- Common attitude toward strangers: If the stranger is recognizable nobility, polite and serious. However, if the stranger is of unkown background, she acts coldly.

**A few personality words:** Reserved, sarcastic and nature-lover.
`Example: reserved, curious, sarcastic, excitable`

> Focus on the personality they _show_. Hidden motivations, fears, history, and deeper traits can stay secret.

## World of Origin

A very short description of where the character comes from.

**World / Setting Name:** Earth, early 19th-century England.

**Brief Description:**  
This character comes from a noble society (Bridgerton inspired).

**One notable thing about their world:** No modern technology nor video games exist yet!

> Enough context to understand why the character might find The Arcade strange without explaining their entire setting.

## Dialogue Flavor

Optional, but useful for interactions.

**Speaking style:** formal and noble. Can get mean when upset.
`Example: formal / casual / blunt / theatrical / quiet`

**Common mannerism or habit:** Has a habit of covering her face with a hand-held fan when when making jokes or is being blunt or mean.

**Something they might say:**

> "Talk to me when you have something meaningful to say."

## First-Impression Hooks

A few things other characters can naturally notice, ask about, or react to.

- Since she is and looks noble, she ias asked about her background.
- People react to how obviously selective she is when choosing who she speaks to.
- She looks posh.

## Creator Notes

Anything collaborators should know when writing scenes involving this character.

**Okay to improvise:** Reactivity, love interests (if any).

**Please avoid changing/revealing:** Her insistance in looking polished and refined, and her nature-lover side.

### Keep Some Cards Hidden

You **do not need to include**:

- Full backstory
- Secret abilities
- Major relationships
- Character arcs
- Trauma or major past events
- Hidden motives
- Plot twists
- Complete lore

Those are more fun to discover through conversations and interactions inside **The Arcade**.
