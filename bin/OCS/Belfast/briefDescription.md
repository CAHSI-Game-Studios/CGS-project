# OC Profile

## Basic Info
**Name:** Belfast  
**Nickname / Alias:** Belfast  
**Pronouns:** He/Him  
**Role / Occupation:** Wandering Scholar / Adventurer  

## Physical Description
A short description of what the character looks like.

- **General appearance:** A sturdy, broad-built duergar dwarf with dark gray skin, dark eyes, long grayish hair, and a thick, well-kept beard.
- **Clothing / style:** Practical traveler’s clothing with a slightly rogue-like appearance, usually including a dark jacket or cloak, sturdy boots, and a well-worn backpack.
- **Distinctive features:** His long beard and imposing build stand out immediately. He often carries a shield and axe, though he is comfortable using whatever tools or weapons a situation requires.

## Displayed Personality
How does the character usually come across to other people?

- **General demeanor:** Calm, confident, observant, and somewhat reserved when first meeting someone.
- **Social habits:** Usually listens and studies a situation before speaking. Once comfortable around someone, he becomes much warmer, more talkative, and playful.
- **Sense of humor:** Playful and occasionally teasing, but usually only around people he trusts.
- **Common attitude toward strangers:** Cautious but respectful. He does not immediately trust strangers, though he is willing to hear them out before judging them.

**A few personality words:**  
`resourceful, intelligent, strong-willed, curious, reserved, adventurous, caring, playful`

## World of Origin

**World / Setting Name:**  
The Forgotten Realms — The Underdark

**Brief Description:**  
Belfast comes from the Underdark, a vast subterranean region of the Forgotten Realms filled with enormous cavern systems, ancient settlements, strange creatures, dangerous ruins, and powerful magic. Life beneath the surface can be harsh and unpredictable, producing people who value awareness, resourcefulness, and self-reliance.

**One notable thing about their world:**  
Belfast is accustomed to navigating sprawling underground environments where darkness, monsters, ancient ruins, and magical phenomena are ordinary parts of travel.

## Dialogue Flavor

**Speaking style:**  
Blunt, straightforward, and slightly old-fashioned. He tends to say what he means without dressing it up unnecessarily, though his tone becomes noticeably warmer around friends.

**Common mannerism or habit:**  
He tends to quietly examine unfamiliar objects, mechanisms, or surroundings before interacting with them, especially if he cannot immediately understand how they work.

**Something they might say:**  
> "Hmph. Strange contraption... Give me a moment. Everything makes sense once you know which part is likely to explode."

## First-Impression Hooks

- His appearance clearly marks him as someone accustomed to living far beneath the surface.
- He seems unusually interested in unfamiliar machinery, objects, books, and anything else he has never encountered before.
- Despite carrying weapons and looking intimidating, he behaves more like a curious traveler or scholar than a typical warrior.

## Creator Notes

**Okay to improvise:**  
His reactions to unfamiliar technology, casual conversations, jokes, minor adventures, scholarly interests, and the kinds of subjects he has encountered during his travels. Belfast is broadly knowledgeable and resourceful, so it is reasonable for him to know a little about many subjects without being an expert in everything.

**Please avoid changing/revealing:**  
Major details about his past, important relationships, hidden motivations, unexplained abilities, or the specific reasons behind his travels. Avoid making him excessively hostile, cruel, or distrustful simply because he is a duergar; while cautious and blunt, Belfast is ultimately kind, curious, and caring.
