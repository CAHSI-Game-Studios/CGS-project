## Physical Description
A short description of what the character looks like.


- General appearance:A 29-year-old woman with fair skin, a slim but fit build, and dark hair threaded with striking white streaks.

- Clothing / style:Dark, fitted clothing with red accents and sturdy boots. She maintains a deliberate, polished appearance despite the wasteland around her.

- Distinctive features:A red eyepatch covers her missing right eye. Together with her white hair streaks, it makes her instantly recognizable.

### Keep Some Cards Hidden 

You **do not need to include**:

- Full backstory
- Secret abilities
- Major relationships
- Character arcs
- Trauma or major past events
- Hidden motives
- Plot twists
- Complete lore

Those are more fun to discover through conversations and interactions inside **The Arcade**.
