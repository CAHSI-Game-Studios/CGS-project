# OC Profile

## Basic Info
**Name:**  Lyra
**Nickname / Alias:**  The Pink "Human"
**Pronouns:**  She/Her
**Role / Occupation:**  Voodoo Dolls Collector

## Physical Description
A short description of what the character looks like.

- General appearance: She has short white hair styled in two small space buns with a golden streak, neon green eyes, and bright pink skin.

- Clothing / style: She wears an alternative style—a red jumpsuit, blue leggings, and large boots. Some of her accessories feature eyes, as they are zombies she converted into items (including her Voodoo keychain). She has a nose piercing, an emerald necklace, and frequently wears black bracelets.

- Distinctive features: She does "girly" poses, usually keeps her eyes closed, and has a small smile that reveals her tiny fangs.

> Keep this brief. Think "what another character would notice after meeting them," not a complete anatomical inventory.

## Displayed Personality
How does the character usually come across to other people?

- General demeanor: She is an extremely outgoing, hyperactive, and energetic girl. She loves being around people and has zero hesitation when approaching strangers.

- Social habits: very but VERY Extroverted. She greets people in multiple languages all at once until they respond, unknowingly invades personal space, and loudly advertises her sidekick services.

- Sense of humor:Similar to Starfire's, featuring clumsy attempts to act human. She often makes fun of how humans do everyday tasks (e.g., drinking water through their mouth instead of their ear).

- Common attitude toward strangers: Warm, effusive, and direct. She treats everyone as if they were already her best "human" friends, though she still gets defensive or aggressive if someone interrupts her explanations about her 200 senses.

**A few personality words:**  
`Example: reserved, curious, sarcastic, excitable`

Curious, analytical, sweet/innocent, impulsive, defensive, expressive

> Focus on the personality they *show*. Hidden motivations, fears, history, and deeper traits can stay secret.

## World of Origin
A very short description of where the character comes from.

**World / Setting Name:**  New Mexico, United States (Planet Earth)

**Brief Description:**  
What kind of place is it? Fantasy, modern, sci-fi, ruined world, strange dimension, etc.

She lives on Planet Earth, which is currently in the middle of a zombie apocalypse. She is the only alien residing on the planet and doesn't have any particular invasion mission; she simply takes advantage of the chaos to use her laser beams to turn zombies into voodoo dolls for her personal collection.

**One notable thing about their world:**  

It is in the middle of a full-blown zombie apocalypse. Human inhabitants don't really know how to defend themselves—some simply give up, while others form large military factions. People usually get quite frightened when they see her use her superpowers (even though she clumsily insists they are completely normal human abilities).

> Enough context to understand why the character might find The Arcade strange without explaining their entire setting.

- Before speaking to you, she first greets you in multiple languages until you react to one.

- She accidentally admits her alien traits or abilities, but tries to fix it with a simple reminder: "I am an ordinary, average human."

## Creator Notes
Anything collaborators should know when writing scenes involving this character.

**Okay to improvise:**  

 - Context / Period Adaptation: You can play with how she interacts with characters from different eras or genres. If she meets someone from medieval times (or any other setting), she will try to "fit in" by inventing absurd habits or "human historical facts."

 - Bonds & Romances: She can develop friendships, crushes, or romantic interests (regardless of gender), as long as they accept or tolerate her addiction to collecting voodoo dolls.

 - Voodoo Doll Gifts (Crushes / Loved Ones): Lyra can transform another defeated character or creature into a functional voodoo doll and gift it to her crush, close friend, or loved one as an extreme token of affection, allowing them to use that doll/character to their tactical advantage!

 - Absurd "Alien" Facts: Feel free to get creative inventing weird anatomical or biological facts that she casually drops as if they were normal (e.g., "I have four ears and two of them are on the soles of my feet. Don't you?").

 - Dynamics & Comedic Moments: You can adapt her personality tone depending on the situation or shift her nervousness/aggression levels to generate comedy.

 - Play Along (or Not): Your character can pretend to believe she is human to avoid conflict, or confront her inconsistencies to see how she clumsily tries to justify them.

 - Sidekick Role: Other collaborators are allowed to use Lyra as a sidekick or companion in their stories or missions, provided the group pays or rewards her with a zombie (or similar creature) so she can turn it into a new voodoo doll.

 - Combat Skill Flexibility: You can use Lyra as a sidekick in any type of adventure or era. She is fully willing to learn new combat skills (such as fencing, hand-to-hand combat, swordsmanship, stealth, or battle tactics) if her "trainer" or team promises a zombie as payment.

**Please avoid changing/revealing:**  

 - Do Not Break the "Humanity" Rule: Prevent her from ever admitting or revealing directly that she is an alien. She will always defend that she is a regular human person, no matter how obvious or ridiculous the opposite is.

 - Keep Threats Comedic and Absurd: If she gets "aggressive," avoid graphic or explicit violence (like breaking bones or eradicating species). She only fires lasers at zombies she wants to turn into items, or fights physically if her opponent started it. Her threats should remain comedic, cute, and harmless (e.g., "I will hit your pinky toe very hard!").

- Full backstory
- Secret abilities
- Major relationships
- Character arcs
- Trauma or major past events
- Hidden motives
- Plot twists
- Complete lore

Those are more fun to discover through conversations and interactions inside **The Arcade**.
