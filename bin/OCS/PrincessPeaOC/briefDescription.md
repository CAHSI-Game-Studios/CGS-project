# OC Profile

## Basic Info
**Name: Princess Pea**
**Nickname / Alias: Lady Pea**
**Pronouns: she / her** 
**Role / Occupation: Medieval Princess** 
## Physical Description
Tall and lanky 17 y/o teenage girl, has long blonde hair with bouncy bangs, purple "almost lilac" eyes and a pointy nose. Her outfit resembles that of Briar Rose frolicking in the forest. Never wears shoes. 


- General appearance: Tall and ethereal, the fairest of all the ladies in Evermoor, has a sense of fairylike quality. She has every typical princess trait and ability, but enhanced to a comedic degree. Ex: her voice is in a singsong matter and enchants people when she talks in her riddles / rhymes, talks to animals like their her friends, etc. 
- Clothing / style: Briar Rose Medieval dress with no shoes and a pointy hat
- Distinctive features: beautifully adorned hair, smells like flowers and music appears out of nowhere. Resembles Aurora in her appearance.

> Characters notice how Lady Pea enchants everyone she encounters, there's a general awe about her. Her hair is always flowing in the wind and light follows her. Music magically plays whenever she appears. 

## Displayed Personality
How does the character usually come across to other people?

- General demeanor: enchanting and charming
- Social habits: lack of special awareness, intrusive nature, ultra optimism
- Sense of humor: she does not have a sense of humor, she takes things very literal and does not understand sarcasm. 
- Common attitude toward strangers: friendly demeanor, almost too friendly. talks in riddles and rhymes to human characters, to anthropomorphic characters she speaks normally. 

**A few personality words:**  
`Example: whimsical, joyful, optimistic, overtly romantic, idealist`

> Lady Pea is very cheerful and romantic, she views the world through rose color glasses. She desperately awaits to be rescued and constantly puts herself in situations that could put her in danger because of the possibility of meeting her prince. She has no awareness of reality and is used to the world folding to her will. 

## World of Origin
Princess Pea belongs to the land of Evermoor; the kingdom is reigned by magic and the power of belief. The land is known for its bright optimism and stereotypical chivalry nature, villagers often romanticize their roles and their humdrum everyday life. The land is habitat to a wide array of magical creatures and anthropomorphic beings that coexist amongst humans.

**World / Setting Name:**  Evermoor Land

**Brief Description:**  
Evermoor is a Medieval kingdom sequestered in the depths of a forest and is powered by belief in magic and optimism. There's a general ethereal atmosphere, the grass is always green and blossoming with flowers, buildings are very quaint and rustic, there are waterfalls and wheelbarrows adorning the village. In general it is a very utopic Medieval world, almost cartoony. 

**One notable thing about their world:**  

> The world although ethereal in its aesthetic, reveals deep contradictions within. The villagers are unaware of how they romantice their everyday lives and their roles in the grand scheme of things. Everyone in Evermoor basically has a role to fill and a character to play, nothing changes, everything is cliche. Once the "arcade player" invades the game, this facade is unveiled and the world starts to slowly shift.
