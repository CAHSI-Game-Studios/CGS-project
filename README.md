## 1. Project Overview

The Arcade is a 2D game with narrative-driven elements that takes players through a variety of unique worlds, each filled with original characters, environments, and experiences.

The story follows an ordinary electrical technician who gets mysteriously sucked into an old arcade machine and must find a way out. As players progress through different levels and worlds, they encounter various characters, discover their stories, and become involved in their lives. What begins as an attempt to escape gradually turns into an adventure where the player has the opportunity to help others, overcome challenges, and become the hero of each world.

Combining platforming, exploration, character interactions, and environmental storytelling, The Arcade aims to create an experience where every world has something new to discover.

### What Makes The Arcade Unique?

- **Original Worlds:** Each world is built around original characters (OCs) created by members of the development team.
- **Different Experiences:** Every world has its own identity, mechanics, characters, and challenges.
- **Narrative-Driven Gameplay:** Players discover stories and develop relationships with the characters they encounter.
- **Collaborative Creativity:** The project brings together programming, art, storytelling, and sound design contributions from different members of the research group.

## 2. Story and Setting

### The Story

The protagonist is an ordinary electrical technician in his mid-30s whose life hasn't quite turned out the way he imagined. Once an aspiring game developer with a passion for video games, he now finds himself working odd jobs to make ends meet.

His latest assignment takes him to Jovanni's Pizza Palace, where he's tasked with repairing an old arcade machine. During the repair, an electrical accident sends him directly into the machine, trapping him inside the mysterious world of The Arcade.

Initially shy, somewhat meek, and overwhelmed by his unfamiliar surroundings, he must navigate the dangers of this strange dimension while searching for a way home. However, as he explores its worlds and befriends their inhabitants, he gradually begins to rediscover forgotten interests, memories, and parts of his personality.

His journey becomes more than an escape: it's an opportunity to reconnect with the person he once was.

### The Arcade

The Arcade is a mysterious pocket dimension hidden within an old arcade machine, believed to be a junction of forgotten games. Rather than being an ordinary machine, it resembles an ancient relic concealing an entire world within its seemingly ordinary exterior.

Inside, the player finds themselves in a mysterious void filled with floating rocks and scattered environments. As they travel through this space, they discover new areas that lead to different worlds, each with its own inhabitants, stories, and challenges.

However, the void is not as empty as it first appears. A mysterious entity watches the player's journey from somewhere beyond the known areas of the Arcade, raising questions about the nature of this place and the reason behind the player's arrival.

## 4. Features and Gameplay

The Arcade combines platforming, exploration, and narrative-driven gameplay. Players begin in the Hub, a central area that serves as the main point of exploration and access to the different worlds.

### Gameplay Features

- **Hub Exploration:** Walk around and explore the central Hub before entering the different worlds.
- **World-Specific Abilities:** Each world introduces its own gameplay mechanics and abilities, allowing the experience to change depending on the game being explored.
- **Character Interactions:** Engage in dialogue with original characters and NPCs to uncover their stories and advance the narrative.
- **World Objectives:** Help characters resolve conflicts, overcome challenges, and save their respective worlds.
- **Token Collection:** Collect tokens throughout the game to unlock progression and gain access to subsequent worlds.

### Gameplay Experience

Each world is designed to offer a different experience. Players can expect changing gameplay mechanics, unique environments, and character-driven stories that encourage exploration and interaction.

The Hub connects these experiences, serving as the central area from which players gradually discover more of the Arcade.

## 5. Development

The Arcade is developed using the Godot Engine alongside various tools for asset creation, audio production, and project management.

### Tools and Technologies

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Godot Engine | Game development and implementation |
| LibreSprite | Pixel art and sprite creation |
| Aseprite | Sprite and pixel art creation |
| Cakewalk | Music and audio production |
| GitHub | Version control, collaboration, and task management |

### Team Organization

Development is divided into two main groups:

#### Programming Team

- Implements gameplay mechanics and systems.
- Develops levels and game functionality.
- Integrates assets and maintains the project's technical components.

#### Art Team

The art team is divided into three areas:

- **Artists:** Create character sprites, environments, and other visual assets.
- **Storyboarding:** Develop narrative concepts, character backgrounds, and world progression.
- **Sound Design:** Produce music and audio elements that establish the atmosphere of the game.

### Development Workflow

The team follows a Scrum-based workflow, organizing regular meetings where members receive assigned tasks and discuss their progress.

GitHub is used to manage development through issues, branches, pull requests, and code reviews. Producers help oversee this process by reviewing pull requests, monitoring issues, and ensuring proposed changes remain within the project's scope.

This structure allows the different teams to coordinate their work while maintaining a consistent development process.

## 6. About CAHSI Game Studio

CAHSI Game Studio (CGS) is a research group operating under the Computing Alliance of Hispanic Serving Institutions (CAHSI) at the University of Puerto Rico at Mayagüez (UPRM).

CGS focuses on providing students with opportunities to explore video game development in an academic environment where hands-on opportunities in this field can be limited.

Through workshops, collaborative projects, and game jams, the group encourages students to develop technical, artistic, and creative skills while learning to work together.

The group brings together students interested in different aspects of game development, including programming, art, storytelling, music, and game design. While programming positions have specific academic requirements, students with relevant experience in artistic and creative disciplines can also contribute.

### About The Arcade Project

The Arcade is a collaborative research project designed to give group members an experience similar to working in a real-world video game studio.

Through the development process, members gain practical experience with:

- Collaborative game development and interdisciplinary teamwork.
- Programming, game design, and asset creation.
- Project management and task organization.
- Creative problem solving and narrative development.
- Version control and collaborative development workflows.

By working together to create an original game, members can apply the knowledge and skills developed within the research group while gaining insight into the processes involved in producing a video game.
